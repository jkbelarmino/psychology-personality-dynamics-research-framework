# Psychology: Personality Dynamics Research Framework
URL: https://jkbelarmino.github.io/psychology-personality-dynamics-research-framework/

A non-diagnostic empirical literature synthesis on personality dynamics, Dark Tetrad constructs, strategic self-presentation, and interpersonal research.

## About

The **Personality Dynamics Research Framework** is an educational research reference that maps generic interpersonal patterns to findings and constructs discussed in peer-reviewed personality and interpersonal research.

The framework is designed to help readers distinguish between:

- Observable interpersonal patterns
- Psychological research constructs
- Findings reported in the academic literature
- Possible interpretations of those findings
- What the evidence does **not** establish about an individual person

It is intended as a literature-synthesis and research-reference resource, not as a diagnostic or assessment tool.

## Scope

The framework covers research relating to areas including:

- Personality dynamics
- Dark Tetrad research
- Narcissistic admiration and rivalry
- Machiavellianism
- Psychopathy-related traits
- Everyday sadism
- Strategic self-presentation
- Interpersonal relationships
- Status and social valuation
- Dyadic relationship research

The framework focuses on correspondence between generic behavioral or interpersonal patterns and constructs described in the research literature.

## Important Boundaries

This website is **not**:

- A psychological evaluation
- A diagnostic tool
- A clinical assessment
- A forensic assessment
- Legal advice
- A legal opinion
- Expert testimony
- A method for determining another person's mental state, intent, character, or legal responsibility

Individual behaviors can have multiple explanations. A correspondence between a behavior and a research construct does not establish that a particular person possesses that construct or meets criteria for any disorder.

Research associations should not be interpreted as proof of causation.

## Evidence

The framework is based on peer-reviewed academic literature and provides source information and DOI links where available.

The cited research is used to explain established research constructs and reported empirical findings. The synthesis itself should not be treated as independently peer-reviewed.

The framework does not establish the truth of a disputed allegation, the mental state or intent of a particular person, or the legal significance of conduct.

## Use of the Framework

The framework is intended to support:

- Academic and personal research
- Literature exploration
- Understanding personality research terminology
- Critical evaluation of interpersonal claims
- More careful distinction between behavior, construct, and inference

It should not be used to diagnose, label, or make definitive psychological claims about real people based solely on observed behavior.

## Sources

The website provides a reference list of the peer-reviewed literature used throughout the framework, including DOI links to primary publications where available.

## Safety & Support

The website includes general safety and support information for readers dealing with potentially harmful interpersonal situations.

If someone is in immediate danger, emergency assistance should be contacted.

The framework emphasizes that a person does not need to determine whether someone else has a psychological disorder in order to recognize that behavior is harmful or to seek appropriate support.

## Disclaimer

This project is an educational literature synthesis and research reference. It does not provide medical, psychological, psychiatric, forensic, or legal advice.

Questions concerning a particular person's health, safety, rights, legal position, or evidence should be addressed to an appropriately qualified professional.
